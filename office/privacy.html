I'm the Boss Now — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information CHRISTANBOOKSTORE, INC., doing business as CT Games ("we", "us") handles when you play I'm the Boss Now on TikTok Minis, why, and the choices you have. Our Terms of Use are a separate document.

1. Who We Are

I'm the Boss Now is a game on TikTok Minis made by CHRISTANBOOKSTORE, INC., doing business as CT Games, a company in Alberta, Canada. We are responsible for the information described in this policy. Contact: admin@chrisbookstore.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect as little as possible. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, followers, photos, messages, precise location or your TikTok profile (nickname or avatar).

  • TikTok login identifier. When you open the game, TikTok signs you in silently (no pop-up). TikTok gives our server an open ID: a random identifier that is unique to you and this game only. We use it to keep your cloud save. We do not ask for any profile permission and we do not keep TikTok access tokens.
  • Cloud save. Your game progress (for example floor, levels, in-game money, team members, outfits, settings and time of the last save) is stored on our server linked to your open ID, so you keep your progress on a new phone.
  • On-device data. The same progress, your settings and a random install ID are also stored on your device.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics. The game sends events such as opening the game, starting or finishing a fight, levelling up, which in-game buttons were tapped (including which optional ad button), session length, app version and the launch source (for example an ad link). These use the random install ID and a session ID, not your open ID, and we only look at them in aggregate.
  • Ads. Ads are served by TikTok. TikTok may collect data for ads under the TikTok Privacy Policy; we do not receive personal data from those ads.

Purchases. The game does not currently sell anything. If we add optional purchases, they will be handled by TikTok and we will update this policy before they appear.

3. How We Use Information

  • To run the game and keep your progress (cloud save).
  • To improve balance, fix problems and understand which features players use.
  • To deliver items you buy, if purchases are offered.
  • To keep the game secure and prevent cheating or abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use it to make automated decisions with legal effects.

4. Sharing

  • TikTok, which runs the platform, login, ads and payments.
  • Our hosting provider (Railway), which stores our server data on our behalf under contract.
  • Authorities, only if required by law.

We do not share your information with anyone else.

5. Where Data Is Stored

Our server data is stored with our hosting provider in the United States. By playing, your information may be processed outside your country, with protections required by applicable law.

6. How Long We Keep It

  • Cloud save: until you delete it, or 12 months after your last save.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics: 12 months.
  • Purchase records (if any): as long as required for tax and accounting law.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Delete your cloud save in the game: Settings → Delete my cloud save (tap twice). This deletes your save from our server immediately; progress then stays only on your phone.

You can also email admin@chrisbookstore.com to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, or to withdraw consent. Depending on where you live (for example Canada, the United States, the EU/UK) you may have these rights by law; we will answer within 30 days and will not treat you differently for using them. Removing the game or clearing its data deletes what is stored on your device.

8. Children

I'm the Boss Now is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will delete the related data.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store only the minimum data above, and limit access to it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top, and for important changes we will show a notice in the game.
