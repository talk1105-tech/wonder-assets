Spades — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CHRISTANBOOKSTORE, INC., doing business as CT Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play Spades on TikTok Minis. Our Terms of Use are a separate document.

1. Information We Collect

Spades is a single-player game. Your progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, TikTok profile or precise location.

Gameplay statistics. To improve the game, it sends anonymous gameplay events to our server: a random install ID created on your device (not linked to your TikTok account), a session ID, and simple events such as opening the game, bidding, starting or finishing a hand or game, the score, using the Coach, opening the rules, and how long the session lasted, plus the launch source (for example, an ad link). We use this only in aggregate and keep it for up to 12 months. It does not include your name, contacts, location or TikTok profile.

TikTok provides us with aggregated, anonymous usage statistics through its developer tools.

2. Ads

The game offers optional rewarded video ads (coach tips, hand reviews, bonus stars and card backs) and occasional ads between levels or games. TikTok serves these ads and may collect data under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

3. Children

Spades is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

4. Deletion

Removing the game or clearing its data deletes what is stored on your device. Anonymous gameplay statistics are deleted after 12 months. You can also contact us with any request.

5. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.
