Euchre — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CHRISTANBOOKSTORE, INC., doing business as CT Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play Euchre on TikTok Minis. Our Terms of Use are a separate document.

1. Information We Collect

Euchre does not send any personal information to our servers. Your game in progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, TikTok profile or precise location.

TikTok provides us with aggregated, anonymous usage statistics (such as the number of players and how long they play) through its developer tools.

2. Ads

The game offers optional rewarded video ads. TikTok serves these ads and may collect data under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

3. Children

Euchre is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

4. Deletion

Because your data stays on your device, removing the game or clearing its data deletes it. You can also contact us with any request.

5. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.
