Euchre — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CHRISTANBOOKSTORE, INC., doing business as CT Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play Euchre on TikTok Minis. Our Terms of Use are a separate document.

1. Information We Collect

Single-player games, your statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, TikTok profile or precise location.

Online play. If you choose Quick Match or Play with Friends, the game connects to our table server and sends a random player ID, a randomly generated display name (for example "Sunny Jack 42"), the table code and your card moves, so the game can be played with other people. Other players at your table see that display name and the short preset messages you choose to send. There is no free-text chat. Table data is kept only in server memory while the table is open and is deleted when the table closes; standard server logs (such as IP address and connection time) may be kept for up to 30 days for security.

Gameplay statistics. To improve the game, it sends anonymous gameplay events to our server: a random install ID created on your device (not linked to your TikTok account), a session ID, and simple events such as opening the game, starting or finishing a hand or game, the score, using the Coach, opening the rules, and how long the session lasted, plus the launch source (for example, an ad link). We use this only in aggregate (for example, how many players finish their first game) and keep it for up to 12 months. It does not include your name, contacts, location or TikTok profile.

TikTok provides us with aggregated, anonymous usage statistics (such as the number of players and how long they play) through its developer tools.

2. Ads

The game offers optional rewarded video ads and occasional ads between full games. TikTok serves these ads and may collect data under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

3. Children

Euchre is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

4. Deletion

Removing the game or clearing its data deletes what is stored on your device. Online table data is deleted automatically when the table closes, and anonymous gameplay statistics are deleted after 12 months. You can also contact us with any request.

5. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.
